│   │   └── TalkSystem.hpp   # Demon negotiation logic and dialogue trees
│   ├── entities/            # RPG Data models

│   │   ├── Demon.hpp        # Recruited demons, fusion compatibility

│       └── BattleHUD.hpp    # HP/MP bars and party panels
